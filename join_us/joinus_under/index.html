Joining Our Group [Undergraduate B2/B3]

For undergraduate students at UoA

Annually, we deliver an internal laboratory introduction presentation. Please attend the presentation, ask questions, and give thoughtful consideration to the topics discussed.

Step 1: Initial Inquiry

Research Interest

  • Review the Principal Investigator’s (PI) research areas and ongoing projects on our website. Check out our publication and patent as well. Ensure that your interests align with our work.
  • Check the laboratory website to learn more about broader research directions.
  • Check out how we work to learn about how we work in our group.
  • Check out what you learn to learn about what you can learn in our group.
  • Check out some case studies to learn about what the students did before.

Contact the supervisor

Email to me with the following:

  • Your GPA and the final grades for the following courses if you took them in the past:
    • C Programming
    • C++ Programming
    • Java Programming I
    • Logic Circuit Design
  • A brief statement of research interests

You can find the supervisor's email at this page.

Email template

If you don't know how to write a proper email, please use this template.

Preliminary Meeting

If your background and research goals match our focus, you may be invited for a virtual (via Zoom) or in-person (if you are nearby us) meeting to discuss potential projects and funding opportunities.

Step 2: Follow the university guidance

After getting the confirmation from me, please follow the instructions of the university.
